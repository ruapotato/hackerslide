# Design audit, 2026-11-24

An audit of everything in the repository at commit `d902836`, done before any further design work. Nothing was
changed during the audit. Each finding says what the design does, what the source document says, and the fix.

**How it was done.** Five independent passes, each reading the primary documents (datasheets, vendor drawings,
the Linux tree) rather than the comments in the code: power chain; audio, storage and connectors; module pins
and socket geometry; keyboard footprints and the TCA8418; enclosure and board placement. Every JLCPCB part code
was re-queried against the JLCPCB parts API. ERC and DRC were re-run.

**Severity.** BLOCKER: will not work, or will be damaged. MAJOR: works badly, or will cost a board revision.
MINOR: should be fixed, low risk. Items marked *(estimate)* are calculations, not datasheet values.

Nothing here has been built or measured. "Verified" means checked against a document, not against hardware.

classic fatal carrier error), and the **charger, boost, codec and microSD pin maps are all correct** pin by pin.

## 2. What checked out

- **ERC/DRC** match the handoff: keyboard 240 unrouted, carrier 449 unrouted, top plate 0; silkscreen warnings
  only; schematic parity clean on all three boards.
- **Part codes:** all 55 LCSC codes on the carrier and keyboard return the stated MPN from the JLCPCB API and
  are in stock. Low stock: `C473397` BQ27441 (86), `C2991787` MWSA0412S-1R0MT (372).
- **Socket placement:** hole pattern 48 x 33 mm, J1A carries pins 1-100, pin 1 at the correct end, odd row
  outer on J1A and inner on J1B. Checked against the Raspberry Pi CM4IO footprint and CM4 datasheet Fig. 4.
- **DF40 land pattern:** 0.2 x 0.70 mm pads, rows 3.08 mm apart, 19.6 mm span. Matches Hirose catalogue p.3 and
  the CM4IO footprint.
- **`cm4_pin_names.json`:** no differences from the CM4 datasheet for all 200 pins.
- **Module pins on Core3566 and Radxa CM4:** 5 V in, 3.3 V / 1.8 V out (600 mA each), pin 78 reference, DSI
  lanes and polarity, USB OTG (true OTG, used for flashing), SD, both I2C buses, debug UART (UART2, 1.5 Mbaud on
  Core3566), reset, activity LED, backlight PWM, all ten I2S straps, every plain GPIO.
- **BQ25895:** all 24 pins and the pad; strapping of OTG, /CE, DSEL, STAT; BTST, REGN, PMID, VBUS and BAT
  capacitor values; TS divider (charging allowed from about 1 to 40 degC at the NTC, *estimate*); L1 rating.
- **TPS61230:** pin map; input range 2.3-5.5 V against a worst-case VSYS of 4.66 V; true output disconnect.
  (The TPS61230**A** is the 4.5 V part. Do not substitute it.)
- **TPS61169:** pin map, 204 mV reference, 20.4 mA with 10 ohm, OVP 36-39 V.
- **USB-C receptacle** pin map and CC resistors; **USBLC6-2** pinout.
- **Power button diode** orientation and the /QON low threshold *(estimate: 0.05-0.25 V against 0.4 V max)*.
- **ES8316:** all 32 pins and the pad; supply voltages; capless headphone output; I2C address 0x10.
- **microSD (Molex 104031-0811):** pin map, footprint, card-detect polarity; 1.42 mm tall.
- **DRV5032 pinout** (the `TODO(verify)` in `carrier_design.py:315` can be closed).
- **Inductor footprints:** all three KiCad footprints match Sunlord's land patterns exactly.
- **Alps SKSWCFE010 footprint:** matches the Alps drawing; two terminals.
- **Diode direction** in the current RP2040 matrix: all 53 pairs conduct column to row, as claimed.

**Fuel gauge swap (approved): MAX17048G+T10**, `C2682616`, 8-TDFN 2 x 2 mm, 25,608 in stock, $1.95.
Pins: 1 CTG = GND, 2 CELL = cell+, 3 VDD = cell+ with 100 nF, 4 GND, 5 ALRT = GAUGE_INT, 6 QSTRT = GND, 7 SCL,
8 SDA, pad = GND. Address 0x36. Mainline `max17040_battery.c`, compatible `maxim,max17048`. Removes RS1, U4,
C18, C19. Limitation: voltage-only, so there is no discharge-current reading anywhere in the system. The TDFN
land pattern still has to be checked against Maxim's drawing.

**Boost headroom.** At the minimum current limit the TPS61230 delivers about 2.3 A at 3.0 V in and 2.6 A at 3.4 V
*(estimate, 90 % efficiency)*. Expect 2 A peaks down to roughly 3.4-3.5 V cell voltage, not near empty.
Alternative with more margin: TPS61089RNRR `C165129` (18,161 in stock, $0.91).

## 9. Thickness

Current stack (mm): tray 5.4, slide gap 0.4, back floor 1.2, cavity 7.8, carrier 1.0, flex gap 0.5, panel 3.93,
bezel lip 0.8. Total **21.03**.

The cavity is 7.8 mm because the 7.5 mm cell sits under the carrier. If the carrier is cut out around the cell,
the cell can use the cavity plus the carrier thickness plus the flex gap:

| Option | Cell | Cavity | Total with the new 4.25 mm panel |
|---|---|---|---|
| As drawn (cell under carrier) | 7.5 mm, 3000 mAh class | 7.8 | 21.4 |
| Carrier cut out, same cell | 7.5 mm, 3000 mAh class | 6.3 | 19.9 |
| Carrier cut out, thinner cell | 6.5 mm, about 2500 mAh *(estimate)* | 5.6 | 19.1 |
| Cell stacked under the module (spec 3.7) | 7.5 mm | 13.2 | 26.8 |

All figures are from the model, with the module at the datasheet's 5.08 mm mated height. LuckFox's drawing
suggests about 4.5 mm, which would take another 0.5 mm off the cut-out options if a real module confirms it.
The 4.5-5.0 mm headphone jack fits a 5.6 mm cavity. No cell has been sourced: the 3000 mAh generic cell has no
datasheet, and P7 requires one rated for 2 A charge.

Body thickness is David's decision.

## 10. Consequences for the keyboard scanner decision

Recommendation: **TI TCA8418** I2C keypad scanner in place of the RP2040 USB keyboard.
`TCA8418RTWR`, `C138713`, 30,228 in stock, $1.18. Mainline `tca8418_keypad`.

- Removes the RP2040, flash and crystal from the keyboard, and the USB hub, mux and second crystal from the
  carrier. Findings M3 and the hub half of the USB sheet disappear.
- The module's single USB port then goes straight to USB-C, which keeps flashing simple.
- The keyboard cable drops from 20 conductors to about 8, which helps E5.
- Costs: no keyboard in the bootloader; Fn layer handled in the Linux keymap; the hub's reserved port for a
  future modem is gone.

Required changes found by the audit:

1. The 5 x 12 matrix does not fit the chip's 8 x 10. Remap to 6 rows x 10 columns: rows 0-3 carry physical
   columns 0-9; row 4 carries physical columns 10 and 11; row 5 is the D-pad.
2. The chip drives columns low and reads pulled-up rows, so every diode must be reversed.
3. Keep the diodes. Without them, Ctrl + Shift + letter reports a phantom key (TCA8418 datasheet 9.1.1).
4. Run it from 3.3 V (the row low level with a diode in series is too high at 1.8 V, *estimate*).
5. The pins are rated 2 kV HBM only. Ground the top-plate copper and add protection at the cable.
6. Footprint: `WQFN-24-1EP_4x4mm_P0.5mm_EP2.45x2.45mm` (the Texas RTW footprint has the wrong pad).
7. The driver has no wake-up code of its own; `wakeup-source` goes through the I2C core and is untested.
